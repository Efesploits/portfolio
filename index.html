    01 — About

    The animator behind the frames

    I'm M3FXN — a Roblox animator obsessed with the two seconds most people skip. The wind-up before the swing. The hitch in a landing. The half-frame hold that makes a punch land.

    I work primarily in Moon Animator for R6 rigs and move into Blender when a shot needs real camera work, simulation or lighting. Everything ships game-ready: clean keys, correct rig hierarchy, looping tested in Studio, exported and documented so your scripters never have to guess.

    Combat sets, movement kits, emotes, weapon rigs, trailer cutscenes — if it moves on a Roblox character, I've probably animated it. I take a small number of commissions at a time so nothing leaves half-polished.
